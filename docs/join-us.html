Join Us

We welcome collaborators and applicants interested in AI-enabled multi-hazard forecasting, early warning systems, and decision support.

Our work combines machine learning, physical modelling, and uncertainty analysis to support risk-informed decisions for natural hazards.

Opportunities

TREES DLA PhD projects — 2027 entry

Applications are open through the TREES Doctoral Landscape Award. The application deadline is 14 December 2026 at 17:00 UK time.

PhD · CASE partnership with BGS

Quantifying Landslide Impacts across Europe using AI

Develop AI and Earth observation methods to map landslides across Europe and investigate their contribution to disaster impacts and multi-hazard risk.

Supervisors: Faith Taylor and Lorenzo Nava, King’s College London.
Co-supervisors: Alessandro Novellino and Ekbal Hussain, British Geological Survey (BGS). Includes a BGS placement of at least three months.

PhD · AI and cascading hazards

Predicting Extreme Geohazard Flows with AI

Combine satellite, terrain, seismic and environmental data to reconstruct and predict long-runout flows, including debris flows and glacial lake outburst floods.

Supervisors: Tom Matthews and Lorenzo Nava, King’s College London.
Co-supervisor: Max Van Wyk de Vries, University of Cambridge.

Research themes

  • Multi-hazard and cascading event modelling
  • Machine learning for hazard detection and forecasting
  • Uncertainty-aware prediction and evaluation
  • End-to-end warning pipelines and decision support
  • Open tools, reproducible science, and real-world impact

Further opportunities

For PhD, MSc, visiting research, and fellowship enquiries beyond the advertised projects above, please contact us using the guidance below.

PhD / MSc students

Applicants with a strong quantitative background (e.g. machine learning, statistics, physics, engineering, or geoscience) and an interest in hazards and resilience.

Postdoctoral researchers

Researchers with evidence of independent work, strong writing, and a clear research agenda aligned with the lab’s themes.

We are happy to support applications to competitive postdoctoral fellowships, including:

  • Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowships
  • Leverhulme Early Career Fellowships
  • 1851 Research Fellowships
  • Newton International Fellowships
  • Royal Society or UKRI-funded schemes (where applicable)

Research software / engineering

Researchers with experience in machine learning tooling, data pipelines, and reproducible research.
